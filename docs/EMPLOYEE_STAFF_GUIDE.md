# ✂️ DarziDesk: Employee, Master Tailor & Staff Complete Guide
*(Master Cutter / Tailor / Karigar / Workshop Artisan Workstation)*

---

## 📌 Employee & Worker Role Ka Purpose Kya Hai?
**Employee / Worker Role** workshop ke core artisans (Master Ji, Cutting Master, Coat Specialist, Trouser Karigar, Hand Embroidery Artisan, aur Store Staff) ke liye design kiya gaya hai. 

Worker ko boutique ke sensitive finance ya accounting dekhne ki zaroorat nahi hoti — unka focus sirf **apne assigned orders ko millimeter accuracy ke sath cut karna, stitch karna, aur deadline par complete karna** hota hai.

---

## 📑 Feature-by-Feature Detailed Hinglish Guide

---

### 1. 🖥️ Worker Personal Workstation Dashboard

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Har master tailor ya karigar ka personalized work screen.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Worker ko dukaan par aate hi pata hona chahiye ki aaj unki table par kitne kapde silne ke liye pending hain aur kiska trial aaj 2 baje hai.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- **Worker KPI Cards**:
  - `Assigned Garments`: Total kapde jo is worker ko diye gaye hain.
  - `Today's Due Work`: Jin kapdon ka trial ya delivery aaj hai.
  - `Completed This Week`: Karigar ne is hafte kitne pieces complete kiye.
- **Daily Performance Trend**: Day-by-day completed vs pending pieces ka graphical chart.
- **Urgent Notification Bar (`notifyOrder`)**: Red flashing alerts for priority wedding/ceremonial orders.

#### 🚀 Workshop Fayda:
Tailor ko bar-bar boutique owner se jakar puchna nahi padta ki *"Bhaiya aaj kya silna hai?"*.

---

### 2. 📇 Digital Job Cards & Workshop Print Tags (`/order/job_card`, `/order/print_tag`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Garment ke sath attach hone wala digital aur printable cutting & stitching instruction sheet.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Boutique me 50 kapde ek sath katte hain. Agar fabric par customer ka tag na ho toh galat lining ya galat button lagne ka 100% chance hota hai.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- **Job Card Contents**:
  - `Order Number & QR Code`: E.g. `#ORD-1049`.
  - `Customer Name & Category`: E.g. "Bespoke 3-Piece Tuxedo".
  - `Fabric & Lining Code`: Matching thread, canvas, and button specifications.
  - `Style Specifications`: Notch Lapel, 2-Button, Double Vent, Ticket Pocket, Hand Pick Stitching.
  - `Trial & Delivery Deadline`: Target dates.
- Master cutter tag print karke fabric roll/cut pieces par pin kar deta hai.

#### 🚀 Workshop Fayda:
0% confusion between fabric rolls and tailoring styles.

---

### 3. 📐 Body Measurement Inspection & Posture Notes (`/measurement/show`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Customer ke exact body measurements aur anatomical posture instructions ko dekhne ka screen.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Master cutter ko pattern kaatne ke waqt exact chest, waist, slope, armhole aur customer ki body shape ka pata hona mandatory hai.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Worker garment card par click karke direct measurement sheet open karta hai:
  - **Jacket / Coat**: Shoulder (18.5"), Chest (40"), Waist (34"), Sleeve (25"), Cross Back (16").
  - **Trouser**: Length (41"), Inseam (31"), Thigh (24"), Bottom Hem (14.5").
  - **Special Posture Notes**: E.g. *"Right shoulder is 0.5 inch lower than left", "Customer stands with stooping posture — give extra back length"*.

#### 🚀 Workshop Fayda:
Fitting ki accuracy 1st try me hi 100% perfect baithti hai.

---

### 4. 🔄 Production Stage Moving (Drag & Status Update)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Jaise-jaise tailor ka kaam aage badhta hai, garment ka status live update karna.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Front desk staff aur customer dono ko real-time pata chal sake ki kapda cut chuka hai ya sil raha hai.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Jab Master Ji cutting complete karte hain $\rightarrow$ Status update hota hai: `Cutting Completed`.
- Jab Tailor silayi poori karta hai $\rightarrow$ Status update hota hai: `Stitched & Ready for Trial`.
- Jaise hi status update hota hai, **Customer ke phone par automatically WhatsApp SMS trigger ho jata hai** ki unka trial ready hai.

#### 🚀 Workshop Fayda:
Workshop aur showroom reception ke beech zero phone calls ya communication gap.

---

### 5. 💵 Personal Piece-Rate Wages & Advance Log (`/tailor-ledger`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Har karigar ka apna personal digital hisab-kitab account.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Karigar ko transparency chahiye hoti hai ki usne is hafte kitne rupaye ka kaam kiya aur kitna advance cash liya.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Tailor apna dashboard open karke dekh sakta hai:
  - Total pieces finished this week (e.g. 5 Suits @ ₹1,500 = ₹7,500).
  - Cash advance taken (e.g. ₹2,000 on Wednesday).
  - Remaining net wage to be received on Saturday: ₹5,500.

#### 🚀 Workshop Fayda:
Karigaron ka trust banta hai aur weekend par settlement 1 minute me clear ho jata hai.
