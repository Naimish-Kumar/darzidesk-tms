# 📱 DarziDesk: Customer Self-Service Portal Complete Guide
*(Boutique Client / Customer Tracking & Measurement Passport)*

---

## 📌 Customer Portal Ka Purpose Kya Hai?
**Customer Portal** boutique ke clients ko ek luxury, high-tech, aur transparent tailoring experience provide karta hai. 

Customer apne mobile phone ya laptop se bina boutique ko call kiye apne bespoke kapdon ka **live stitching status track kar sakta hai, digital measurement passport dekh sakta hai, aur PDF bills download kar sakta hai**.

---

## 📑 Feature-by-Feature Detailed Hinglish Guide

---

### 1. 🔍 Live Garment Lifecycle Progress Tracker (`/my/orders`, `/my/orders/{id}`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Bespoke tailoring ka live tracking progress bar — thek waise hi jaise Swiggy ya Amazon par delivery track hoti hai.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Customer ko wedding ya festival ke kapdon ke liye chinta rehti hai ki unka suit time par ready hoga ya nahi.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Customer `/my/orders` par login karta hai ya invoice SMS me diye link ko click karta hai.
- Ek visually stunning progress timeline dikhti hai:
  $$\text{1. Order Booked} \longrightarrow \text{2. Pattern Cutting} \longrightarrow \text{3. Hand Stitching} \longrightarrow \text{4. Trial Fitting} \longrightarrow \text{5. Ready For Pick-Up}$$
- Har stage ka exact date/time aur status message show hota hai (e.g. *"Your Italian Wool Tuxedo is currently in Master Pattern Cutting stage"*).

#### 🚀 Customer Fayda:
100% peace of mind aur zero tension.

---

### 2. 📐 Digital Measurement Passport (`/my/measurements`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Customer ke anatomical body dimensions ka secure digital cloud vault.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Customer jab future me dubara order dena chahta hai, ya online koi outfit customize karwana chahta hai, toh unke paas unka verified body measurement hamesha available rehta hai.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Customer apne saved garments (Suits, Blazers, Sherwanis, Shirts, Trousers, Blouses) ke exact measurements dekh sakta hai (e.g. Chest 40", Waist 34", Sleeve Length 25").
- 1-click me **Measurement Passport PDF** download ho jata hai.

#### 🚀 Customer Fayda:
Har baar dukaan par jakar napwayi (measurement) dene ki jhanjhat khatam.

---

### 3. 🧾 Digital Invoices & Payment Receipts (`/my/invoices`, `/my/invoices/{id}`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Boutique ke sabhi past bills, advance payment slips, aur outstanding balance ka digital record.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
Paper receipts kho jati hain aur delivery ke waqt kitna advance diya tha iska proof nahi milta.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Table me har bill ka breakup dikhta hai:
  - Total Order Cost (e.g. ₹18,500).
  - Advance Paid (e.g. ₹10,000 via UPI).
  - Remaining Balance Due (e.g. ₹8,500).
- GST Compliant PDF Invoice instantly print ya download kiya ja sakta hai.

#### 🚀 Customer Fayda:
100% payment transparency aur tax invoice backup.

---

### 4. 📅 Master Tailor Fitting Appointment Booking (`/tailor/{id}`)

#### 🎯 Kya Hai? (What is this?)
Boutique ke Master Tailor ke sath in-person consultation ya trial fitting appointment online book karne ka portal.

#### 💡 Kyun Zaroori Hai? (Why this feature?)
High-end bespoke studios me customer ko bina waiting ke VIP private fitting lounge experience mil sake.

#### ⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (How it works?)
- Customer boutique profile (`/tailor/1`) open karta hai jisme master tailor ka experience, craftsmanship guarantees, fabric mills (Loro Piana, Zegna, Banarasi Silk), aur showroom photos dikhti hain.
- Customer date, preferred time aur required service (e.g. Bespoke 2-Piece Suit, Wedding Sherwani) select karke appointment book karta hai.
- System instantly **Booking Reference Number** generate karke boutique ko notify kar deta hai.

#### 🚀 Customer Fayda:
VIP premium atelier experience without waiting in long queues.
